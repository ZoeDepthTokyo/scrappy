# Troubleshoot Scrappy

**When to use:** When Scrappy does not start, connect, search, or show expected results.

**Copy-paste prompt:**
> Diagnose my Scrappy problem. Read AGENTS.md first and follow its safety rules. Use `Scrappy.exe --doctor`, the local `/health` route, and `/run_meta` as appropriate. Ask me for symptoms and non-sensitive error text only. Do not ask for credentials or private listing/backup data. Ask before changing data or settings.

**Expected result:** The likely cause and a safe next step are identified without disclosing private data.

**If it fails:** Stop before changing or deleting data. Give me the exact non-sensitive error to investigate and refer to AGENTS.md Troubleshooting.
