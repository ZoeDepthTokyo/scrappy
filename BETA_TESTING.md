# Beta testing Scrappy

Thanks for trying Scrappy. You do not need technical experience. Please share what felt clear, confusing, or slow.

## Try Scrappy

1. Download `Scrappy.exe` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ZoeDepthTokyo/scrappy/releases).
2. Open it. Windows may show SmartScreen because this beta is unsigned. If you are unsure what to do, stop and ask for help; you do not have to continue.
3. For your first search, choose **No AI** and use the default search criteria. Select **Run Now** and wait for listings to appear.
4. Open the Kanban board and try moving a listing between stages.
5. Choose **Quit**, reopen Scrappy, and check whether your listings and board choices are still there.
6. Optional: in Settings → AI, use **Sign in** to connect with OpenRouter. You can skip this and continue using No AI.
